A short exposure reduces motion blur but darkens the picture; use bright light.

For moving objects use bright light: the camera then chooses a shorter exposure (less motion blur). A high frame rate alone does not guarantee short exposures.

Zoom uses the camera's own zoom. Beyond the lens's optical range it is digital (it crops the picture and adds no detail). Keep zoom and phone position unchanged between calibration and measurement.
